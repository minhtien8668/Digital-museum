MASTERPIECE

GIRL WITH A PEARL EARRING

Johannes Vermeer c. 1665
Oil on canvas 44.5 × 39 cm

ABOUT THE ARTWORK

Girl with a Pearl Earring is one of Johannes Vermeer's best-known paintings. Created around 1665, the work presents a young girl wearing a blue and yellow headscarf and a large pearl earring. The soft lighting and simple dark background create an intimate and timeless atmosphere.
