MASTERPIECE

THE STARRY NIGHT

Vincent van Gogh 1889
Oil on canvas 73.7 × 92.1 cm

ABOUT THE ARTWORK

The Starry Night is one of Vincent van Gogh's most famous paintings. Created in 1889, the work shows a night sky filled with swirling stars, a quiet village, and a tall cypress tree. Van Gogh used expressive brushstrokes and strong colors to create a sense of movement across the sky.
